# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on building machines and software capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## 1. Machine Learning

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

There are three main types of machine learning:

- **Supervised Learning:** The model learns from labeled data. Examples include spam detection and house price prediction.
- **Unsupervised Learning:** The model discovers patterns in unlabeled data. Examples include customer segmentation and clustering.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** An agent learns by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties.

## 2. Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers. It is widely used in image recognition, speech processing, natural language processing, and autonomous systems.

Popular deep learning frameworks include PyTorch and TensorFlow.

## 3. Large Language Models

Large Language Models (LLMs) are AI models trained on large amounts of text and other data. They can perform tasks such as answering questions, summarizing documents, generating code, and translating languages.

Examples of LLMs include GPT, Gemini, Llama, and Claude.

LLMs generate text by predicting the next token based on the context they receive.

## 4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines information retrieval with text generation. Before answering a question, a RAG system retrieves relevant information from an external knowledge source and provides it to the language model.

A typical RAG pipeline includes document loading, text splitting, embedding generation, vector storage, retrieval, and answer generation.

RAG is useful for building chatbots that answer questions from company documents, research papers, educational material, and technical manuals.

## 5. Python for AI

Python is a popular programming language for artificial intelligence because it has a large ecosystem of libraries.

Commonly used libraries include:

- NumPy for numerical computing.
- Pandas for data manipulation.
- Matplotlib for data visualization.
- Scikit-learn for traditional machine learning.
- PyTorch for deep learning.
- LangChain for building applications powered by language models.

## 6. Applications of AI

Artificial Intelligence is used in many industries:

- Healthcare: assisting with medical image analysis and research.
- Education: personalized learning and automated tutoring.
- Finance: fraud detection and risk analysis.
- Transportation: route optimization and autonomous driving research.
- Customer service: chatbots and automated support systems.

## 7. Limitations of AI

AI systems can make incorrect predictions, produce misleading information, and reflect biases present in their training data. Their output should be evaluated carefully, especially in high-impact situations.

Data privacy, security, transparency, and responsible use are important considerations when developing AI applications.

## Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence continues to transform the way people work, learn, and solve problems. Understanding Python, machine learning, deep learning, and LLM technologies provides a strong foundation for developing practical AI applications.
